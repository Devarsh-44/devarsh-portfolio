Brand Guidelines

The identity system for RCintel, a systems and UI/UX design studio for telemetry, motorsport and HMI interfaces.

01Brand essence
RCRacing
intelIntelligence

RCintel designs the screens that drivers, engineers and pit walls read at 300 km/h: telemetry dashboards, HMI and data-heavy product interfaces. The brand looks the way that work should feel. Calm, exact and quietly alive.

Precise

Engineered spacing, clean geometry, nothing decorative without a job.

Premium & quiet

Restraint over noise. Matte colour, foil accents, generous space.

Alive

A small character and a row of shift lights add a pulse: data with a personality.

Voice. Speak like a race engineer on the radio: short, specific and confident. Say what the design does for the driver or the client. Skip hype words.

02Logo system

One signature, a small family

The Signature is the logo. The other marks support it in places where it doesn't fit. Use the simplest mark that works, and never place two marks side by side.

03The character

A capsule with a point of view

  1. The capsuleThe shape of an HMI indicator or a status pill on a dashboard. It is the core of the system.
  2. The eyesThe intelligence: always watching the data. Slate with a small catch-light so it feels alive, never cartoonish.
  3. The outlineA fine slate edge keeps the character crisp at small sizes and separates it from the badge.
  4. The floating shadowA soft shadow under the capsule gives matte depth. In the flat version it is removed.
04Brand motif

Shift lights

Eight lights from a steering-wheel display: four lit in foil, two in blue-gray, two off. It sits above the monogram and works as a pattern across the whole brand.

Progress & loading

Case-study progress, loading states, proposal stages.

Section divider
Case study

A short row of lights or a rule with a title between chapters.

Rules

Always horizontal, always 8 lights, always in this order. Lit lights use Foil or Almond, never another colour.

05Clear space & size

Give it room

Keep a margin equal to x, the width of the R's upright stroke, on every side of the Signature. Nothing else, text or image, goes inside that zone. The same rule applies to the other marks.

Signature100 px · 28 mm wide
Horizontal lockup180 px · 45 mm wide
Badge24 px · 8 mm
Badge, full detailfrom 64 px
Monogram40 px · 12 mm wide
Watermark8–12% of image width

The script is fine, so below 100 px switch from the Signature to the Monogram. Below 64 px use the flat badge. Watermarks sit in a corner at 40–60% opacity.

06Colour

Matte blue-gray, warm almond, deep slate

A cockpit palette: cool instrument blues against warm leather tones. Every file in the kit uses only these eight values. The two Blue-Gray tints exist so the script stays readable; Champagne Foil is a finish for special moments, not a fill colour.

09Usage

Keep it consistent

Use the supplied files. Never retype the wordmark or redraw the marks.
Place marks on Night Slate, Off-White or Almond, or on calm photography with enough contrast.
Use the flat badge below 64 px and the flat monogram for watermarks.
